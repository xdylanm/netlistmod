# 0016 — Behavioural expressions

- **Status:** proposed (M15)
- **Date:** 2026-11-06
- **Context:** M15 adds the behavioural source B and the expression forms of E, F, G, H, C and L on both dialects. An expression may read nets and sources that are not in the element's node list (§7.8). Phase 2 plan, section 8, point 1, decided that expression edits are allowed, that the reads stay out of the signature, and that a read-only query reports them. Probes on ngspice 47 and LTspice 26.1.1 are in [m15-tasks.md](../design/m15-tasks.md) section 3. The maintainer accepted the recommendations on 2026-11-06.
- **Decision:**
  1. **Split.** M15 adds B, the expression forms of E, F, G and H (ngspice `vol=`, `cur=` and `value`; LTspice `value=`), ngspice C `q=`, LTspice C `Q=`, L `Flux=` and the hysteretic L, the formula scan, the expression edit and the query. M15b adds TABLE and `table=`, POLY, `Laplace=` (E, G and B), and ngspice's FREQ and AND, OR, NAND and NOR, with decision 0017. Until then those forms stay `UnsupportedConstruct`.
  2. **Where a bare expression ends.** An expression that is not in quotes or braces runs to the line end or to the first trailing parameter of its element: ngspice B `tc1=`, `tc2=`, `temp=` and `dtemp=`; LTspice B `ic=`, `tripdv=`, `tripdt=`, `Rpar=`, `vprx=` and `laplace=`. Any blank followed by a name and `=` (not `==`) ends it, so an unknown parameter is refused rather than read into the expression. It may hold blanks, `{…}` and `+` continuations, and is one grammar terminal per physical line (P13); the text is kept as written, continuations included. ngspice's `vol=` and `cur=` need quotes or braces, as its manual writes; bare, they stop ngspice and are refused.
  3. **Node classes.** `BehavioralSource` (B) has `quantity` (`"v"`, `"i"`, and on LTspice `"r"` or `"p"`), `expression: Formula` and `params`. The expression forms of E, F, G and H keep their classes (`Vcvs` and the rest): `ControlledSource.value` becomes `Value | None`, and the class gains `expression: Formula | None` and `form` (`"vol"`, `"cur"` or `"value"`). Exactly one of `value` and `expression` is set. `Cccs.control` and `Ccvs.control` become `ElementReference | None`: LTspice's `value=` form has no controlling source. A bare expression is a `Formula` with `delimiter=""`.
  4. **Behavioural C and L.** `Capacitor.charge: Formula | None` (ngspice `q=`, LTspice `Q=`) and `Inductor.flux: Formula | None` (LTspice `Flux=`); `value` is then `None`. The hysteretic L is an `Inductor` with no value and `Hc=`, `Bs=`, `Br=`, `A=`, `Lm=`, `Lg=` and `N=` as parameters. ngspice's `R=`, `C=` and `L=` expressions stay formula values (M3).
  5. **The formula scan.** In an expression the scan reads `{…}` inside a bare expression, `+` continuations and LTspice's `~`. The words an expression gives a meaning to are not parameter references there: LTspice's `time` in B and in `value=`, and `x` in `Q=` and `Flux=` (`s` in `Laplace=` is M15b's). Elsewhere, LTspice's `time` stays an ordinary name, as M8 found. The scan reads names; it never evaluates.
  6. **The query and its diagnostics.** `Netlist.expression_reads(designator, scope=())` returns a tuple of `ExpressionRead(kind, name, span, target)`, in source order: `kind` is `"node"` for each name in `v(a)` or `v(a,b)` and `"source"` for `i(Vx)`; `target` is the `Net` or the element, or `None`. A node read resolves in the element's own scope, through ground, `.global` and the subcircuit's ports. A node no element connects to is `UNRESOLVED_NODE`, a new code: a warning, or info where the scope has an `.include`, `.lib` or opaque block. A missing source is `UNRESOLVED_ELEMENT`, as for F's controlling source; LTspice's `I(X1:Vq)` is info (§7.8). ngspice's `i()` may name any device, so there is no mismatch check. The reads stay out of the signature.
  7. **Edits.** `set_expression(designator, text)` replaces an expression's text and keeps its delimiters as written: braces, quotes or none. It covers B, the expression forms of E, F, G and H, C's charge, L's flux, and an R, C or L formula value. The quantity and the form are not editable. The trailing parameters use `set_param`. `set_value` on an expression form is an `EditError` that names `set_expression`. A bare expression whose new text would not read back as one expression (for example one that holds ` name=`) is refused by verification.
  8. **POLY's control nodes are topology, counted from the dimension** (M15b). After `POLY(N)` the transformer takes the first 2N names as control nodes (E, G), or the first N as controlling sources (F, H); fewer is a `ParseError` at the line's end.
  9. **Refusals.** Refused (decision 0002): ngspice B `r=` and `p=`, a second quantity, and no expression, all of which stop ngspice; on LTspice a second quantity, no expression, `Rpar=` on a `V=` or `P=` B (LTspice stops: parallel resistance is for current sources), ngspice's `vol=` (stops) and `TABLE {…} =`, which LTspice runs but its reference does not show (recorded in `discrepancies.md`). ngspice's `LAPLACE` stays a `ParseError`: the manual says ngspice does not offer it, and ngspice stops. FREQ and AND, OR, NAND and NOR are M15b's.
  10. This record amends §5.3 (`BehavioralSource`, the new fields), §7.2 (`set_expression`), §7.8 (expression reads and the query) and decision 0006 (`UNRESOLVED_NODE`). The rules and operations are in the family modules `model/_rules_behavioral.py` and `edit/_ops_behavioral.py` (P12).
- **Consequences:**
  - The device matrices show B as `supported`. NG-BEH-001, LT-BEH-001, NG-NLS-001 and LT-NLS-001 are new spec entries; NG-CAP-001, LT-CAP-001, LT-IND-001, NG-UNS-001 and LT-UNS-001 change.
  - A caller that read `ControlledSource.value`, `Cccs.control` or `Ccvs.control` must allow `None`.
  - The signature snapshot is unchanged. The tree snapshot adds the new files, and the same 17 rejected files that M13 and M14 changed list the B letter among their expected terminals.
  - The M15b corpus files are in the families now, marked `pending` in their manifests, and the loaders and solver sweeps skip them with that reason.
  - The stale-citation check no longer rejects §5.1.1 and §5.2.1 to §5.2.4: in the manual edition in `references.md` they are the B and E source sections.
