# 0019 — Transmission lines

- **Status:** proposed (2026-11-09; M16, accepted when M16 closes)
- **Date:** 2026-11-09
- **Context:** M16 adds the transmission-line letters: T (lossless) and O (lossy, LTRA) and U (uniform RC, URC) on both dialects, and ngspice's Y (TXL) and P (CPL), with the `.model` forms their models need. The maintainer decided section 2 of [m16-tasks.md](../design/m16-tasks.md) point by point on 2026-11-09; this record carries those decisions. The probes behind them are in section 3.1 of that list and in `tests/solver/test_ngspice_line_probes.py` and `tests/solver/test_ltspice_line_probes.py`, on ngspice 47 and LTspice 26.1.1.
- **Decision:**
  1. **Nodes.** A public base `TransmissionLine(Component)` and one class per letter: `LosslessLine` (T; no extra field), `LossyLine` (O), `RcLine` (U), `TxlLine` (Y) and `CoupledLine` (P), the last four with `model: ModelReference`. T's `Z0=`, `TD=`, `F=`, `NL=` and `IC=` (a `ValueList`), U's `l=` and `n=`, and Y's and P's `len=` are `params`. `port1` and `port2` give each port's nodes (on P, a port's X nodes and then its reference node), `conductors` gives P's X, and U has three nodes and no ports. *Considered:* one class for all five.
  2. **T's parameters** are a closed list per dialect: ngspice `Z0=`, `TD=`, `F=`, `NL=` and `IC=`; LTspice `Zo=` and `Td=`. `Z0=` must be given, and exactly one length form: `TD=`, or `F=` with or without `NL=`. A line without a length, `TD=` beside `F=`, and `NL=` without `F=` run in both solvers and are refused. ngspice's `IC=` is exactly four comma-separated values. `rel=` and `abs=` (both solvers), and on LTspice `F=`, `NL=` and `IC=`, run and are refused, each with its own message.
  3. **`Z0` and `Zo` are both accepted, on both dialects, as one parameter.** The manual writes `Z0=` and the reference `Zo=`, and both solvers read both; LTspice's own `tline` symbol writes `Z0=`. A line that writes both, or one of them twice, is a `ParseError` at the second, so an ambiguous impedance never loads. `set_param` and `remove_param` follow the line's spelling. This is a deliberate exception to decision 0002 (solver behaviour does not widen the manual), not a general relaxation.
  4. **O, U, Y and P instance fields.** O takes nothing after its model. U takes `l=` (required: ngspice runs without it at a near-zero length, LTspice stops) and an optional `n=`, in either order; a positional length is refused. Y and P take an optional `len=`; `m=` and a positional length are refused. `l=`, `n=` and `len=` are values in the dialect's value slot: a number, or a formula in braces or quotes. On LTspice a bare name (`L=len`, `Zo=z`), which LTspice reads as a parameter reference, is refused on U and T as on every other element, with a message suggesting `{len}` (review R2).
  5. **The reference nodes of Y and P must be ground** (`0` or `gnd`, any case), in a subcircuit too: ngspice ignores them, so any other name would put a connection into the signature that the solver does not make. The check is in the model layer, so the ground names move from `document/names.py` to `dialects.py`, which both layers may import, with no change in behaviour. *Considered:* accepting them as a known limit (§7.8 is for what the library cannot see).
  6. **P's nodes and model** follow decision 0014's boundary rule: the model is the last name before `len=` or the line's end, and a number there is refused. The nodes are an even number from 4 to 18 (one to eight lines); `port1` is the first half. T1 confirmed the limit: ngspice 47 runs eight lines and crashes on nine with a valid matrix.
  7. **ngspice U is a URC line when exactly four names** come before its first `name=value` or the line's end; every other shape, a type with parentheses included, is the digital U of M17 and raises `UnsupportedConstruct` ("ngspice digital U device"). Five names whose last is a number or `off` are refused as a URC line's positional length or flag. On LTspice U is only URC. ngspice U is `supported` with milestone `M16, M17`.
  8. **LTRA's flags** (`nosteplimit`, `nocontrol`, `lininterp`, `mixedinterp`, `truncnr`, `truncdontcut`, and `steplimit` from the manual's example; each one word) are `ModelStatement.flags` of an ngspice LTRA model, with or without parentheses, and a `ParseError` on any other type, where ngspice ignores them. T1: `lininterp` and `mixedinterp` change a `.tran` run; no probe decides the others, which load as the manual lists them. On LTspice they are refused (the reference lists none), although LTspice runs three.
  9. **CPL matrices.** After `R=`, `L=`, `G=` or `C=` of a `.model … CPL`, a list of values, with blanks or commas and over `+` lines, held as a `ValueList` from the first value to the last. A list on any other parameter or type is a `ParseError` at the second value. The number of values is not checked. A list over `+` lines may hold a comment at a line's end, the only list that can; its span then holds the comment, which item 11 keeps (review R3).
  10. **Model types.** `LossyLine: [LTRA]` and `RcLine: [URC]` on both dialects, `TxlLine: [TXL]` and `CoupledLine: [CPL]` on ngspice, with `MODEL_TYPE_MISMATCH` and `set_model`'s check.
  11. **Edits.** `set_param` sets T's, U's, Y's and P's parameters (`IC=` as a whole list) and refuses O's; a result that breaks items 2 to 4 is refused by verification. `set_model` covers O, U, Y and P; `set_model_param` covers the new models, CPL lists included, and leaves LTRA's flags as written; `set_value` on a line is an `EditError` naming the right operation. Two new operations: `remove_param(designator, name)` on every line where `set_param` sets a named parameter, and `remove_model_param(model, name)`. The removed span follows where the field sits on its physical line (review R1): after another field on its line, the field goes with the separator before it (first inside `.model` parentheses, with the separator after it); first on its line with a later field or the `)` on that line, with the separator after it; alone on its line, from the line break that ends the line before (with a `\` continuation's backslashes and the blanks before them), so that a comment after it moves to the end of the line before, joining a comment already there. A comment only ends a physical line, so no removal of a field with one value touches one, and none is refused for a comment; the last parameter in parentheses leaves `()`. The one field that can hold a comment is a CPL list over `+` lines (item 9); as decision 0017 item 6 does for `set_table`, `set_model_param` with as many values replaces each value's span, so the comment stays, and another count, and `remove_model_param` of the list, are `EditError`s that name the comment (review R3). A value keyword, a positional area, a flag, a missing parameter, an X line's parameters and an O line are `EditError`s naming the right operation; a required parameter is refused by verification. A T's length form is switched in one `batch()`, which works in any order of its operations.
  12. **The planned letter in tests** is A, which stays planned until M17.
  13. **Corpus.** The maintainer's KiCad TLINE export (an O with LTRA) and LTspice T, O, O reject and U schematics with their exports, credited to xdylanm, MIT, and variants that change one form each. The LTspice re-export test covers the family.
  14. **Where the code goes.** The rules are in `model/_rules_lines.py`, the removals in `edit/_ops_remove.py`, and the other line edits in `edit/_ops_elements.py` (P12).
- **Consequences:**
  - This record amends architecture §5.3 (the new nodes, the `ValueList` of a CPL matrix and the LTRA flags in `ModelStatement.flags`), §6.3 rule 14 (P uses the boundary rule), §7.2 (the model types, `remove_param` and `remove_model_param`) and decision 0014 item 7 (flags beyond `pchan` and `nchan`, and the removal operations). Item 3 is recorded as an exception to decision 0002.
  - NG-TLL-001, NG-LTR-001, NG-URC-001, NG-TXL-001, NG-CPL-001, LT-TLL-001, LT-LTR-001 and LT-URC-001 are new spec entries; NG-MOD-001, LT-MOD-001, NG-UNS-001 and LT-UNS-001 change. Every refusal a solver runs has a row in that dialect's `discrepancies.md`.
  - The signature snapshot is unchanged. The tree snapshot loads the KiCad file and adds the M16 files, and 17 rejected files list the five new letters among their expected terminals.
  - T1's probes found three things the task list did not have, recorded in the ngspice discrepancies: ngspice 47 stops on `m=2` on P (the planning probe had it run); a CPL model needs every matrix and `length=` even when the instance writes `len=`, and a TXL model needs `g=` (model contents are not checked, so `remove_model_param` may remove one); and ngspice crashes, rather than stopping, on nine coupled lines.
