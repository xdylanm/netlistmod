# 0017 — Tables, polynomials and transfer functions

- **Status:** accepted (2026-11-09, maintainer; M15b T12)
- **Date:** 2026-11-08
- **Context:** M15b adds the nonlinear forms that M15 left `UnsupportedConstruct` (decision 0016 item 1): TABLE and `table=`, POLY, `Laplace=` and B's `laplace=`, and ngspice's FREQ and AND, OR, NAND and NOR. Decision 0016 already sets three rules: item 7 names the edits, item 8 the counting of POLY's control fields, and item 9 puts FREQ and the gates in M15b. The probes behind everything else are in [m15b-tasks.md](../design/archive/phase-2/m15b-tasks.md) section 3.1, on ngspice 47 and LTspice 26.1.1, and in `tests/solver/test_ngspice_nonlinear_probes.py` and `tests/solver/test_ltspice_nonlinear_probes.py`. The maintainer decided each open point of that list on 2026-11-08 and approved the rest as recommended.
- **Decision:**
  1. **Node fields.** The forms keep the M14 classes (`Vcvs`, `Vccs`, `Cccs`, `Ccvs`). `value` is `None`, and `form` is `"table"`, `"poly"`, `"laplace"`, `"freq"`, `"and"`, `"or"`, `"nand"` or `"nor"`. `ControlledSource` gains `table: Table | None` (TABLE, `table=`, FREQ and the gates' two points), `dimension: int | None` (POLY's or a gate's `N` as written) and `coefficients: tuple[Value, ...]`. `Table` has `points: tuple[TablePoint, ...]`, `layout` (`"pairs"`: each point in its own parentheses; `"flat"`: one parenthesised list), `format: tuple[str, ...]` (FREQ's words as written, empty otherwise) and `span`; `TablePoint` has `values: tuple[Value, ...]` (two, or three for FREQ) and `span`. `expression` keeps one meaning, the formula the line writes: the input of `TABLE {expr}` and `FREQ {expr}`, and the transfer function of `Laplace=`. The four-node TABLE, `table=`, POLY and the gates read their control nodes, so `expression` is `None` there. `window=`, `nfft=` and `mtol=` on E and G, and `m=` on ngspice G and on F and G POLY, are in `params`. On B, `laplace=` stays a trailing parameter whose value is the formula as written, with `window=`, `nfft=` and `mtol=` beside it. *Considered:* `TablePoint(x, y)` with a separate type for FREQ's triples (two shapes for `set_table`), and a separate `laplace` field on E and G (a second formula slot that `set_expression` would have to choose between).
  2. **`control` is a tuple.** `Cccs.control`, `Ccvs.control` and `CurrentSwitch.control` become `tuple[ElementReference, ...]`: the one source of the linear form, the N sources of POLY in line order, and `()` for LTspice's `value=` (decision 0016 item 3 gave `None`). This is a breaking change to the public API, made now because no caller depends on it yet; the CHANGELOG says so. The signature, `UNRESOLVED_ELEMENT`, `CONTROL_SOURCE_MISMATCH` and the reference index loop over `control`. The signature has one `("control", scope, designator, source)` entry for each source, in line order (decision 0015 item 3): a linear line gives the same entry as before, so no digest changes, and swapping two POLY sources changes the order of the entries, which is a `TopologyViolation`. *Considered:* a second field `controls` beside `control`, and one signature entry per line holding a tuple (it would change every saved digest of a netlist with F, H or W).
  3. **Counting.** POLY's `N` is an integer literal of at least 1, with or without a blank before `(`; any other text, `POLY({1})` included (both solvers run it), is a `ParseError` at it, because the dimension decides the topology and must be readable without evaluation. On E and G the transformer takes the first 2N names after `POLY(N)` as control nodes, after the output pair in `nodes`, and the rest as coefficients; on F and H the first N as controlling sources. With fewer, it raises a `ParseError` at the line's end. LTspice's `(n+,n-)` pairs are grouped by the grammar and count as one pair; they may be written with a comma or a blank, tight or not, and mixed with flat names, but it must start on a pair boundary (LTspice stops on `POLY(2) vx (in,0) …`, a pair that follows an odd number of names, while `vx in 0 …` runs); a controlling source in parentheses stops LTspice and is refused. ngspice pairs in parentheses run but the manual does not show them: refused. A gate's inputs are counted from the line, never from `N`, because ngspice ignores `N` and reads every name before the first point as an input: an even number of at least four, and `2N`, otherwise a `ParseError`. *Considered:* accept parenthesised pairs on ngspice, and any `N` on a gate.
  4. **Fields and minimums.**
     - *ngspice TABLE (both forms):* the expression is in braces or quotes (a bare one runs and reads something else, 4.0 for 2.67, so it is refused); `=` is optional; points are pairs or one flat list, with commas or blanks; at least one point, two values each and an even count in a flat list; the values are numbers (the manual says parameters are not allowed, and ngspice reads some forms wrongly), with units allowed; the four-node form is E's only; `m=` follows G's points and is refused on E.
     - *LTspice `table=`:* one parenthesised list, with commas or blanks, over `+` lines; at least two pairs and an even count; pairs in their own parentheses are refused; the values follow LTspice's value slot; no `m=`; four nodes.
     - *POLY coefficients:* the values follow each dialect's gain slot (a bare name is refused); at least two on ngspice and one on LTspice, as a `ParseError` at the line's end (LTspice runs none, as a gain of 1, which its reference does not show).
     - *FREQ:* the expression in braces or quotes, `=` optional, at least one format word, then triples in pairs layout or one flat list; G takes it without `m=`. A unit suffix on a frequency loads as other numbers do (ngspice runs it). Two refusals follow ngspice's own stops and misreadings (review round 1, 2026-11-09): a first frequency, braced or not (`({f0}, …)` stops too), of more than one character as written that follows its `(` directly (`(1e7, …`, `(1.0e+07Hz, …`, the manual's own example; a blank after the `(` runs), and format words that contradict each other (`DB` with `MAG`, `DEG` with `RAD`, `R_I` with any other word: ngspice keeps the last and reads the points by it). A repeated word loads. The message of the first gives the fix, `( 1e7, …)`.
     - *Gates:* E only; exactly two points, each `(x, y)` with a comma, separated by a blank.
     - *LTspice `Laplace=`:* the formula is bare or braced, a bare one ending at the first ` name=`; `window=`, `nfft=` and `mtol=` follow it; any other name, one of these before `Laplace=`, a `V()` or `I()` in the formula, and two nodes are refused. *B's `laplace=`* refuses a `V()` or `I()` too (LTspice stops on both), and follows `V=` or `I=` (refused after `R=` and `P=`); `window=`, `nfft=` and `mtol=` without `laplace=` are refused on B, E and G.
  5. **A net named `table`** (any case) stops ngspice in every node position of E and G, as `value` does (decision 0016 item 9): refused at the node by the transformer, with the message that names the keyword. `poly`, `freq`, `and` and `or` run, and all of these run on LTspice.
  6. **Edits.** `set_table(designator, points)` replaces the points of TABLE, `table=`, FREQ or a gate: `points` is a sequence of points, each a sequence of value texts. With the same number of points only the values are replaced; otherwise the points are written in the layout already on the line, reusing as written the text between the first two values, between the first two points, and between the first point's `(` and its first value (`,`, `, `, a blank, several blanks or none; `( 1e7, …` keeps its blank). When the count changes, a comment inside the table's span is an `EditError`, and over several lines each point must stand on its own physical line, in which case each new point is written on a continuation line like the first (the same prefix, `+` or `+ `); otherwise the edit is refused and suggests changing the points one for one. `set_poly_coefficients(designator, values)` replaces the coefficients from the first to the last; with the same count only the values are replaced, otherwise the separator already there is kept, and a comment among the coefficients, or a continuation line in the run, blocks a change of count. `EditError` before verification: a point of the wrong width, fewer points than the form takes (two for a gate or `table=`, one for ngspice TABLE and FREQ; a gate takes exactly two), a value that is not a number on ngspice TABLE, and fewer coefficients than the dialect takes. The dimension, the control nodes and sources, a gate's inputs, the form and FREQ's format words are not editable (`TopologyViolation`). The expression of TABLE and FREQ and the `Laplace=` formula use `set_expression`; `window=`, `nfft=`, `mtol=`, `m=` and B's `laplace=` use `set_param`; `set_value` on these forms is an `EditError` that names the right operation.
  7. **The scan and the query.** The expression of TABLE and FREQ is scanned as M15's are and joins `expression_reads`. `s` in `Laplace=` and `laplace=` is not a parameter reference (decision 0016 item 5). `Laplace=` can hold no `V()` or `I()`, so it adds no reads. The four-node TABLE, `table=`, POLY and the gates read only their control nodes, which are in `nodes` and the signature.
  8. **Unchanged refusals.** ngspice `LAPLACE` stays a `ParseError`. On LTspice, ngspice's `TABLE {…} =` stays refused.
  9. **Where the code goes.** The rules are in `model/_rules_nonlinear.py` and the edits in `edit/_ops_nonlinear.py` (P12). This record amends §5.3 (the new fields and types), §7.2 (the two operations), decision 0015 (`control` becomes a tuple on F, H and W, with one signature entry per source), and decision 0016 item 3 (`Cccs.control` and `Ccvs.control` are `()` rather than `None` on `value=`) and item 8 (`control` rather than `controls`, the minimums and the gate count).
- **Consequences:**
  - NG-TBL-001, NG-PLY-001, NG-XSP-001, LT-TBL-001, LT-PLY-001 and LT-LAP-001 are new spec entries; NG-NLS-001, NG-UNS-001, LT-UNS-001, and the E, F, G and H entries of both dialects change. Every refusal that a solver runs has a row in that dialect's `discrepancies.md`.
  - A caller that read `Cccs.control`, `Ccvs.control` or `CurrentSwitch.control` must read a tuple.
  - The signature snapshot is unchanged. The tree snapshot gains the new corpus files and loads the M15b ones that M15 wrote as rejected files.
  - T1's probes found three things the task list did not have, recorded in the discrepancies: ngspice runs `m=` after an E's POLY; it stops on a FREQ table whose first frequency follows its `(` directly and has more than one character (the manual's own example; the planning round took this for a `Hz` stop, and a `Hz` suffix runs anywhere); and a net named `table` stops G as well as E. Review round 1 (2026-11-09) also found that ngspice keeps the last of contradicting FREQ format words, that LTspice stops on a POLY pair that does not start on a pair boundary, and that `V()` and `I()` stop B's `laplace=` as they stop `Laplace=`.
