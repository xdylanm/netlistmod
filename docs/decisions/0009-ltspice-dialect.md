# 0009 — The LTspice dialect

- **Status:** accepted (2026-11-02, maintainer; the section 2 decisions of [m8-tasks.md](../design/archive/m8-tasks.md) were accepted on 2026-10-31, and this record, with the points settled in T1–T13 and the review rounds, after the checkpoint B review)
- **Date:** 2026-11-01
- **Context:** M8 makes `Dialect.LTSPICE` a working dialect for the phase 1 constructs, with the same guarantees as ngspice. Architecture §6, §8, §9.1, §10.3, §11.1, §12 and §21 were written with one dialect built, and name some things that the LTspice reference and LTspice 26.1.1 do not bear out. Evidence: probes on LTspice 26.1.1 (Windows, 2026-10-31 and 2026-11-01; m8-tasks.md section 7, [discrepancies.md](../grammars/ltspice/discrepancies.md)). Only syntax facts are recorded (item 2).
- **Decision:**
  1. **Reference.** `analogdevicesinc/ltspice-reference` (Apache-2.0) is the LTspice manual for §11. Citation form: `[ltspice-ref] <FILE>.md "<heading>"`. Commit, access date and the installed copy are in [references.md](../references.md). Paraphrase only (§11.3 rule 4). The built-in LTspice Help is proprietary: it is not cited or copied.
  2. **Licence of evidence** (§12). The LTspice EULA restricts publishing benchmark or comparison results and treats its documentation as confidential. The repository records only **syntax facts**: what LTspice accepts or rejects and how it reads a value. LTspice raw files, logs, timing and performance numbers are never committed; LTspice reader tests use synthetic files written here. Exports of schematics authored here remain allowed. This is the maintainer's call and is not legal advice.
  3. **Grammar composition** (§6.1). `core.lark` holds what both dialects share, and compiles on its own. What only ngspice has moved to `ngspice.lark`: the `\\` continuation, the comma separator (except inside `v(a,b)`), RKM terminals and the `rkm` gate, the keyword and model forms of R, C and L, the `params:` rejection, bare-expression rejection, reserved parameter names, `.nodeset all=`, plain-name and `@dev[p]` `.save` vectors and the AC vector functions, and `.meas sp`. Each field rule gains its ngspice gap tokens with `%extend`. The single-character logical operators `&` and `|` are LTspice's only, in the overlay `formula_ltspice.lark`: ngspice 47 stops at them (review R4). Regression gate: every ngspice test and corpus result is unchanged, including error positions and expected terminals (`tests/grammar/ngspice/test_tree_snapshot.py`, generated before the split).
     Lark inlines sub-terminals at import, so an overlay overrides each name terminal, not `_WUNIT` (settled in T2).
  4. **`.end`** stays required with only blank lines after it, for LTspice too, although the reference allows omitting it and ignores text after it. This is a **deliberate exception to §11.3 rule 2**, to revisit after the phase 1 review: edit placement and the round-trip tests depend on `.end`. `.end` may carry an end-of-line comment in LTspice (the reference allows `;` anywhere).
  5. **Dot-commands** (§6.5). The LTspice overlay has a closed list, unlike ngspice: the typed directives; `.backanno` and `.func` as `UnknownDirective`, also in a subcircuit body; `.four`, `.noise`, `.tf`, `.fra`, `.wave`, `.net`, `.savebias`, `.savestate` and `.loadstate` as `UnknownDirective`, top level only (LTspice stops at them in a body). Anything else is a `ParseError` ("not an LTspice dot-command"), including `.control`, `.if`, and the undocumented `.print`, `.plot` and `.width`, which LTspice tolerates. `.machine` … `.endmachine` is an `UnsupportedConstruct`. `.include`, `.inc` and `.lib` are opaque.
  6. **Numbers** (§6.4). The embedded decimal is native: digits, a multiplier (`T G Meg K mil m u µ μ n p f`, and the observed `R`), digits. `M` is milli. It is accepted in **every** value slot (settled by probe per slot in T5: R, C, L, V, I, K, D area, every parameter, `.param`, `.tran`, `.ac`, `.dc`, `.temp`, `.ic`, `.step`, `.model`). No `rkm` option: `rkm=True` with `Dialect.LTSPICE` is a `ValueError`. The node records `notation="rkm"`; `DIALECT_GATED_SYNTAX` and `RKM_SOLVER_MISMATCH` are not used for LTspice. A number's trailing letters may be any Unicode letters (`1kΩ`).
  7. **Element parameters.** Each letter has a **closed list**, the reference's (LTspice stops at any other name, "Unknown parameter"): R `tc` (a list, its own rule and a `ValueList`), `temp`, `m`; C `ic`, `Rser`, `Lser`, `Rpar`, `Cpar`, `RLshunt`, `m`, `temp`; L `ic`, `Rser`, `Rpar`, `Cpar`, `m`, `temp`, `tc1`, `tc2`; V `Rser`, `Cpar`; D positional area, `off`, `m`, `n`, `temp`. I takes `load` or `R=value`. Widened by the maintainer (review round 1, 2026-11-01), because parts from LTspice's own component database must parse; each name is verified by probe and is `observed`: R `tol`, `pwr`, `tc1`, `tc2`, `mfg`, `pn`, `noiseless`; C `V`, `Irms`, `mfg`, `pn`, `type`, `noiseless`; L `Ipk`, `mfg`, `pn`, `noiseless`; D `area=`, `Rpar=` (review rounds 1 and 2). The flags `noiseless`, `load` and `off` may take a number or a formula, which the reference documents; the text is kept and the threshold is not interpreted. `mfg`, `pn` and `type` take a double-quoted string (blanks and commas allowed) or a bare word, kept verbatim. The lists stay closed: `Rsr=1` fails. X keeps open names, with or without `params:`. R, C and L have **no model form** in LTspice (no such `.model` type; a bare name in the value's place is rejected with a hint) and no keyword form. K with three or more inductors is valid: three and four run when each has its own load. An inductor loop reproduces the symptom of probe L13, whose circuit is not recorded, so that cause is an inference.
  8. **Names** (§8). Bare words in a formula (`scoping.md` S11; maintainer, checkpoint B): only LTspice's built-in values (`pi`, `BOLTZ`, `ECHARGE`, `PLANCK`, `KELVIN`, `GMIN`, `temp`) are reserved, and a definition in scope masks them; a function name, `time` and `freq` are ordinary names when bare, and a name followed by `(` is a call. LTspice compares names by Unicode case folding after NFC (`document/names.py`, by the parse result's dialect, which `ParseResult.dialect` carries). `0` and `GND` in any case are ground; `00` is not. A name excludes only `= ( ) , { } ' " | ;` and blanks (settled by probe). `$G_*` nodes are global by name.
  9. **Comments.** `*` and `;` start a comment line, and `;` an end-of-line comment anywhere. `$` starts an end-of-line comment only with a space on both sides (`observed`; a tab does not count; settled by probe), so `$G_x` stays a name. `//` is not a comment in LTspice. All trailing comments are preserved.
  10. **Encoding** (§5.1, decision 0001). The source layer stays dialect-neutral. Validated on the exports: UTF-8 without a BOM. BOM-less UTF-16LE (caller passes `encoding="utf-16-le"`) and `cp1252` before Latin-1 are recorded in `discrepancies.md`.
  11. **`.step`** (§6.5) has a typed node, `StepDirective`: `param name`, a source, `temp` or `type model(param)`; `lin`, `dec`, `oct` or none; a range, a `list` of at least two values, or `file=`. `directives.set` replaces the `.step` that sweeps the same quantity, otherwise adds one. No limit on nesting: the reference states none and four nested `.step` lines run (settled by probe), so a fourth is not refused. A parameter swept by `.step param` counts as defined.
  12. **Other directives.** LTspice forms: `.tran` with one argument and modifiers; `.ac list`, `.ac file=`; `.dc` with a variation per sweep, up to three sweeps, `list` or `file=` on the last; `.temp` with a list; `.param x 3k` and bare expressions (the reference documents them); `.opt`; `.save` device currents and wildcards; `.meas` without an analysis keyword, and with `noise` or `op`; `.model` `ako:`, and a quoted string or a bare word for a `.model`'s `mfg` and `type` only, kept verbatim and never read as a formula or a reference; LTspice reads a bare word in any other parameter as a parameter reference, so `Is=x` is a `ParseError` that suggests `{x}` (reviews R2, R8); `.ic I(L...)=value`, which the reference documents, indexed like `.save I(...)`, with `I(R1)` kept out by the grammar (review R3). Option names are not checked: the reference's list is partial (settled in T4).
  13. **Device matrix** (§6.7). R, C, L, V, I, D, X and K are supported; A, B, E, F, G, H, J, M, O, Q, S, T, U, W, Z, `@` and `&` are planned for phase 2. N, P and Y are not LTspice letters. `Ö` is not in the reference.
  14. **Fixtures.** `tests/corpus/ltspice/contributed_examples/` holds the maintainer's schematics with their `-netlist` exports, path comment normalised; a local test re-exports and compares. Hand-written families: `elements_basic`, `statements_basic`, `names_basic`, with `_rejects` families.
  15. **Harness** (T9, checkpoint B): `tests/support/ltspice.py` runs `LTspice.exe -b -ascii`; the raw reader moves to `tests/support/raw.py`. A run fails on a non-zero exit code or a `file(line): message` line. An AC analysis writes its bias point to `<name>.op.raw`, so a run holds both files, and (c) compares both without `Title:` and `Date:`. A manifest's `solver` field may also be `{"warning": "<text>"}`: LTspice writes `V1 a 0` (no value) as a warning in the form of an error line. `--require-solver` may be repeated.
  16. **Suites on both dialects.** `tests/document` and `tests/edits` run once per dialect (`tests/support/dialects.py`); a test or a case whose text only one dialect reads is marked `ngspice_only` or `ltspice_only`. LTspice-only grammar tests are in `tests/grammar/ltspice/`.
  17. **Edit conventions** are those of decision 0007 for LTspice too: lowercase keywords, `.param x=1k`, the line terminator of the insertion point, `.include` for `add_include`. `set_param` also edits V and I parameters, a diode's area, positional or written `area=`, and a flag's value in place (`off` becomes `off=1`). `set_model` and `remove_value` on an R, C or L line are an `EditError` that says LTspice has no such model (review R6).
  18. **Reviews:** one task list with two checkpoints: A after T8 (offline), B after T13 (solver validation).
  19. **Node names into an instance** (§7.8, §17; maintainer, review R11). LTspice connects `<instance>:<node>` to that node inside an instance of the same scope. The library does not model the join: the net keeps its text, and `NODE_INTO_INSTANCE` flags it, a warning, or info when the definition is in an include. `a:b` stays a legal name. The name of `X1` is `x1` and of `X§U1` is `u1` (settled by probe).

  **Amendments to the architecture:** §6.1 (core holds the shared subset); §7.8 and §17 (a node name that reaches into an instance); §6.3 rule 2 and §6.5 (the `.end` exception, the closed dot-command list, `.step`); §6.4 (LTspice numbers); §6.7 (`@` and `&`, not `Ö`); §8 (dialect-aware names); §9.1 (the export switch is verified); §10.3 (LTspice harness); §11.1 (citation form); §12 (reference licence, evidence policy); §21 (the CLI export is verified).

- **Consequences:**
  - The library rejects some forms LTspice runs. Each is a row in `discrepancies.md`; the largest group is undocumented parameters and tolerated commas.
  - A stricter LTspice than ngspice on dot-commands and parameter names: a misspelt `.tarn` or `Rsr=1` fails at load.
  - `fold`, `is_ground`, `net_name`, `parse_formula`, `reserved_words`, `build_formula_parser` and `device_matrix` take a dialect; `ParseResult` carries it.
  - A parameter's value may be a `ValueList` or an `Identifier` in LTspice; each has a `text`, so `param.value.text` stays uniform (review R5).
  - Every `LT-*` entry is `validated` on LTspice 26.1.1, with its observed parts in its notes (§11.3 rule 3). Rule tables follow the same rules.
